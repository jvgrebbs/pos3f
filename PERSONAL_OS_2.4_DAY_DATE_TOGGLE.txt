PERSONAL OS 2.4 — DAY / DATE TOGGLE

Implemented:
- Toggle between DAY and DATE display modes on the Today planner.
- DAY mode emphasizes the weekday (e.g. Saturday).
- DATE mode emphasizes the calendar date (e.g. Aug 15, 2026).
- Previous/next day controls operate in either mode.
- Both modes use the same selected date internally.
- The selected date remains synchronized with program week and daily activities.
- Display preference persists locally.
- No program, execution, adaptive, or historical data is changed by toggling.
