PERSONAL OPERATING SYSTEM
PHASE 5 — NUTRITION SYSTEM
Release: v0.5

Implemented:
- Daily breakfast, lunch, and dinner assignment.
- Breakfast uses the fixed low-cost/easy meal.
- Lunch uses a small rotation.
- Dinner uses a 12-week rotation with varied dinners.
- Dinner recipes are constrained to quick meals (30 minutes or less).
- Daily meal completion tracking.
- Weekly grocery "What to Buy" list.
- Grocery item completion tracking.
- Date and week navigation refreshes the nutrition view.
- Existing Phase 1–4 functionality preserved.
- No cloud backup or external synchronization introduced.
