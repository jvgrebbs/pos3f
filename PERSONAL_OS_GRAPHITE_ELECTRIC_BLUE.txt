PERSONAL OS — GRAPHITE + ELECTRIC BLUE THEME

Primary:
- Graphite background: #101317
- Surface: #181d23
- Secondary surface: #20262e
- Borders: #303842
- Primary text: #f2f6fa
- Secondary text: #9aa6b2
- Electric blue accent: #19a7ff
- Blue hover: #4dbbff

Semantic status:
- Success: #35c878
- Warning: #f2b84b
- Danger: #ff5f69

Design intent:
- Graphite is the dominant canvas.
- Electric blue is reserved for active navigation, primary actions,
  focus states, progress and selected/current context.
- Status colors remain semantic and restrained.
- Cards use layered graphite surfaces instead of white panels.
- The theme applies to both the clean UI and the underlying execution
  dialogs so the application remains visually consistent.
